Fabric twisted smoothly around Anastasiya's ankle as she climbed the silks toward the rigging point, testing her grip before attempting the drop sequence she'd been refining for weeks. The circus company's new show opened in ten days.

Her spotter, Desmond, stood ready below with crash mats positioned precisely beneath her practice area, watching her climb with the focused attention that came from years of aerial coaching. "Wrap looks secure," he called up. "You're clear whenever you're ready."

Anastasiya settled into her starting position, checking the fabric's wrap around her ankle one final time before releasing into the controlled drop that would unravel the silk in a dramatic spiral. "Dropping," she called, and let herself fall.

The silk unwound in a smooth, controlled spiral exactly as choreographed, and Anastasiya caught her position perfectly at the sequence's final point, suspended gracefully several feet above the mats. Desmond exhaled the breath he'd been holding, grinning up at her.

"That drop looked incredible," he said. "Cleanest execution I've seen from you yet." Anastasiya climbed back up to reset for another attempt, adrenaline still coursing through her from the successful sequence. "Let's do it again," she said. "I want it perfect for opening night."
