Harness clipped to the safety line, Cassius descended beneath the bridge deck on the inspection rig, flashlight beam sweeping across the steel girders for any sign of the corrosion reported in last year's survey. Traffic rumbled steadily overhead, muffled through the concrete above him.

His inspection partner, Rosalind, operated the rig's controls from the platform above, lowering him slowly while monitoring the tension gauge on the support cables. "How's it looking down there?" she called through the radio clipped to her vest.

Cassius examined a suspicious rust patch closely, running gloved fingers along the steel's surface to gauge how deep the corrosion had penetrated. "Surface rust mostly," he radioed back. "But there's a section here that needs closer measurement, might be worse than the last report suggested."

Rosalind noted his findings on her tablet, marking the exact girder location on the bridge's digital schematic for the maintenance team's follow-up work. "Got it logged," she said. "Move to the next support column when you're ready, we've got six more to check today."

Cassius finished his measurements and signaled for the rig to move, continuing his methodical progression along the bridge's underside as the afternoon sun began its slow descent toward the horizon. "Copy that," he said. "This job never gets less nerve-wracking, dangling under a bridge."
