Morning light filled the studio as Beatrix wedged the last block of clay, pressing air bubbles out with practiced rhythm before centering it on her wheel for the season's final commission piece. This vase would complete a client's ten-piece dinnerware set.

Her apprentice, Cornelius, cleaned the finished pieces waiting on nearby shelves, wiping away stray clay dust before they could be boxed and shipped to the waiting client across the country. "Everything else is packed," he said.

Beatrix centered the clay and began opening it into a cylinder, her hands finding the familiar rhythm that a decade of practice had made almost automatic despite the piece's demanding proportions. "This one needs to be perfect," she said.

Cornelius watched her raise the walls steadily, admiring the control she maintained even on this final, most important piece of the entire commission. "It's looking exactly like the sketch," he observed, comparing it to her design.

Beatrix finished shaping the vase and set down her tools, satisfied with the completed set now ready for its final glazing before shipment. "Last one done," she said. "Let's get this whole set ready for the kiln."
