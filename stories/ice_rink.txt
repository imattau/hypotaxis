Blades scraped sharp lines across the ice as Hana ran through her warm-up routine, breath fogging in the cold arena air. The regional finals were only an hour away, and the stands were already filling with spectators bundled in scarves and mittens.

Her coach, Deshawn, leaned against the boards with a clipboard, watching her footwork with a critical eye. "Your entry into the spin is still a half-beat late," he called out. "Land the jump, then wind up immediately. Don't give the judges a hitch to notice."

Hana nodded and skated to the center of the rink, resetting her posture before launching into the sequence again. This time the jump landed clean, and she spun into the combination without the telltale pause that had cost her points at regionals last year.

"Better," Deshawn said, allowing himself a small nod of approval as she coasted to a stop in front of him, chest heaving. "Now do it again with the music, because a quiet rink doesn't tell you anything about how you'll handle the real thing."

Hana skated back to center ice as the first notes of her program began playing over the arena's speakers, the small crowd's murmur fading into the background as she found her opening position. Deshawn watched from the boards, arms crossed, already counting the beats under his breath.
