Dark chocolate flowed smoothly across the marble slab as Cordwainer worked it with a metal scraper, coaxing the couverture through the precise temperature curve that would give the finished chocolate its characteristic glossy snap. Tempering demanded exact patience.

His apprentice, Ottavia, monitored the digital thermometer, calling out readings as Cordwainer worked the chocolate across the cool marble surface in smooth, methodical strokes. "Eighty-nine degrees," she said. "Getting close to working temperature."

Cordwainer continued working the chocolate, feeling its consistency shift subtly beneath his scraper as the temperature dropped toward the range where proper crystal formation would occur. "Almost there," he said. "The texture changes right before it hits temper."

Ottavia watched the thermometer's reading drop the final few degrees, calling out the number the moment it hit the target Cordwainer had been working toward this entire session. "Eighty-eight point five," she announced. "That's temper range."

Cordwainer tested a small sample on parchment paper, watching it set with the glossy shine and satisfying snap that confirmed successful tempering before moving to mold the full batch. "Perfect temper," he said. "Now we can finally start filling these molds."
