Aiko stood alone on the rain-soaked train platform, clutching a folded letter. Three years had passed since she had last read it. Her hands trembled as she unfolded the damp paper. She looked up sharply as a train horn sounded in the distance. "That whistle," she whispered.

A young man stepped off the arriving train, scanning the crowd on the platform. His name was Ren, and he had not seen Aiko in three years. "Aiko?" he called out. She froze, the letter still in her hand, rain dripping from her hair. "You came back," she said quietly, tears mixing with the rain on her cheeks.
