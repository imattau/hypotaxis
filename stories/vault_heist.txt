The service corridor beneath the bank hummed with the distant thrum of generators as Nadia knelt before the junction box, wire cutters glinting under the beam of her headlamp. Somewhere above them, three floors up, a security guard's footsteps echoed across marble tile.

"Two minutes until the patrol loops back," her partner Ellis murmured through the earpiece, his voice tight with tension. He crouched at the corridor's far end, watching the stairwell door through a small mirror angled around the corner.

Nadia snipped the third wire and the corridor lights flickered once before steadying again. "Cameras are looped," she whispered back, already moving toward the reinforced door at the corridor's end. Her fingers flew across the keypad, entering the sequence they had spent three weeks memorizing.

The lock disengaged with a heavy clunk, and the vault door swung inward on silent, well-oiled hinges. Rows of safety deposit boxes gleamed under the harsh fluorescent light, and Nadia moved straight to the one marked with a small scratch near its lock, exactly as their contact had described.

"Ninety seconds," Ellis warned, still watching the mirror. Nadia's hands stayed steady despite the countdown, sliding a thin pick into the lock and twisting until it gave way with a soft click. She lifted the box's lid and stared for a long moment at what lay inside before snapping a photo with a small concealed camera and easing the lid shut again, leaving everything else exactly as she had found it.
