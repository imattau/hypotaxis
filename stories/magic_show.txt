Backstage curtains rustled as Illusionist Kavi checked the false bottom of his top hat one final time, the muffled roar of the audience building beyond the stage. His assistant, Noor, adjusted the clasps on a mirrored box wheeled into position at center stage.

"Full house tonight," Noor said, glancing through a gap in the curtain. "Even the front row's packed." Kavi rolled his shoulders and tucked a deck of cards into his sleeve with a practiced flick of his wrist, testing the release mechanism one last time.

"Then let's not disappoint them," he said, flashing a grin that didn't quite reach his eyes. A stagehand gave a thumbs up from the lighting booth, and the curtain began its slow rise as the theater lights dimmed to a single spotlight.

Kavi strode onto the stage with practiced confidence, sweeping his cape back as applause washed over him. Noor took her position beside the mirrored box, smiling out at the crowd while her hands worked the hidden latch behind her back without a single visible movement.

"For my next illusion," Kavi announced, voice carrying easily to the back row, "I will need a volunteer brave enough to disappear." Hands shot up across the audience, and Noor swept the mirrored box's door open with a flourish, revealing the seemingly empty interior bathed in soft blue light.
