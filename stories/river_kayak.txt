Whitewater churned around the boulders as Saoirse dug her paddle deep, angling the kayak through the narrow gap before the current could pin her sideways against the rock. Spray soaked her jacket, cold even through the summer heat radiating off the canyon walls.

Her guide, Tobias, called out directions from the raft trailing a few lengths behind, his voice barely carrying over the roar of the rapids. "Line's good, keep your weight forward through the drop," he shouted. "Big hole coming up on your left, stay right of it."

Saoirse adjusted her angle at the last second, cutting hard to avoid the churning hydraulic that would have flipped her without warning. The kayak punched through the standing wave beyond it, and she let out a whoop of relief as the water calmed into a wide, glassy pool.

Tobias steered the raft alongside her a moment later, grinning as he raised his paddle in a mock salute. "That's the best line I've seen through there all season," he said. "You've got maybe two more rapids like that before we hit the takeout."

Saoirse wrung water from her sleeve and grinned back, already scanning the canyon ahead for the next stretch of white water visible around the bend. "Good," she said. "I was starting to think the easy part was over too soon."
