Sediment clouded briefly as Osei finned carefully through the flooded cave's narrow passage, reel unspooling steadily behind him while his dive light revealed limestone formations untouched by surface light for millennia. This expedition sought to map an entirely new section.

His survey partner, Beatrix, followed at a careful distance, sketching the passage's dimensions onto a waterproof slate while monitoring their remaining gas supply against the planned turn-around point. She signaled toward an unexpected chamber opening to their left.

Osei paused at the chamber's entrance, checking his line reel's remaining length against how much further they could safely explore before needing to begin their return journey. "We can go maybe fifty more feet," he signaled. "Then we turn back regardless."

Beatrix nodded and followed him into the chamber, her light revealing a ceiling covered in delicate formations that suggested this space had remained completely sealed from surface water for an extraordinary length of time. She sketched quickly, documenting the discovery.

Osei checked his gas gauge one final time and signaled the turn, both of them beginning the careful reverse journey along their guideline back toward the cave's distant entrance. New chamber logged, he thought, already anticipating the expedition team's excitement.
