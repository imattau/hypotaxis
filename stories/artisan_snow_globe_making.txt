Tiny miniature landscape pieces lay arranged across Wilhelmina's workbench as she positioned the ceramic figures inside the glass dome, working to create the exact scene her client had specifically commissioned. Custom snow globes demanded careful composition.

Her apprentice, Bartholomew, prepared the glycerin solution that would fill the globe, mixing it to the precise ratio that would control how slowly the artificial snow would settle once the piece was completed. "Solution's mixed and ready," he said.

Wilhelmina examined her miniature scene's composition, adjusting a small figure's position slightly before declaring herself satisfied with the arrangement's visual balance. "This looks right," she said. "Let's get the base sealed before filling."

Bartholomew helped her seal the dome's base, working carefully to ensure a watertight connection that would prevent any future leaking once the globe was completed and filled with the glycerin solution. "Seal looks solid," he confirmed.

Wilhelmina carefully filled the globe through its access point, watching the artificial snow settle gently around her miniature scene before sealing it permanently shut. "Perfect," she said. "This one turned out even better than I imagined."
