Frozen breath clouded around Torbjorn's face as he harnessed the lead dogs, checking each connection point along the gangline before the team's morning training run across the frozen lake. Racing season approached, and conditioning couldn't be rushed.

His handler partner, Seraphina, walked the line checking each dog's paw condition, applying protective wax where needed before the cold run across the exposed ice and snow. "Everyone's paws look good," she said. "Ready when you are."

Torbjorn finished the final harness check and climbed onto the sled's runners, giving the team a moment to settle before releasing the brake for their scheduled training distance. "Team's looking eager," he said, watching the dogs' excited anticipation.

Seraphina stepped back from the team and gave a thumbs up, ready to time their run and track the training data that would inform this season's racing strategy and conditioning progress. "Clock's running," she called out. "Good run, everybody!"

Torbjorn released the brake and the team surged forward across the frozen lake, the dogs' powerful strides eating up distance as months of careful conditioning finally paid dividends. "That's what we've been working toward," he called back happily.
