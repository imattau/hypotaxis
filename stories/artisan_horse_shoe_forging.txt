Coal fire roared as Percival heated another iron bar, preparing it for the traditional hand-forging process that would transform raw stock into a custom horseshoe fitted specifically to this particular working draft horse's unusual hoof shape.

His apprentice, Marisol, held the horse's lead rope steady, keeping the massive animal calm while Percival worked nearby with the glowing hot metal and heavy forging hammer. "She's staying nice and calm today," Marisol observed.

Percival drew the glowing iron out and began shaping it against his anvil, checking the developing curve against the hoof measurements he'd carefully taken before beginning this custom forging process. "Getting close to the right shape," he said.

Marisol continued soothing the horse with quiet words and gentle strokes, watching Percival's hammer work with the appreciation of someone who understood how much skill traditional farriery actually required. "That curve looks perfect already," she said.

Percival quenched the finished shoe with a satisfying hiss of steam, examining the cooled metal's shape against the hoof one final time before beginning the fitting process. "Let's see how she likes it," he said. "Should be a perfect fit."
