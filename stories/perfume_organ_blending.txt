Rows of amber bottles lined the perfumer's organ as Wilhelmina selected another aromatic component, adding precise drops to the testing beaker while consulting the formula sketched in her leather notebook. The client's commission demanded a scent evoking a specific childhood memory.

Her assistant, Cassius, prepared testing strips in advance, labeling each one methodically so they could track which combination produced which effect during this session's extensive trials. "Formula seven's ready for testing," he said, handing her a freshly dipped strip.

Wilhelmina inhaled the strip slowly, letting the scent develop through its opening, heart, and base notes before making any judgment about its success or failure. "Closer," she said. "But the vanilla's overpowering the jasmine we need more present."

Cassius made a note of her assessment and prepared the next adjustment, reducing the vanilla component slightly while increasing the jasmine absolute according to her direction. "Formula eight coming up," he said, measuring the components with practiced precision.

Wilhelmina tested the new formula and felt something shift in her chest, the scent finally capturing the elusive quality she'd been chasing through seven previous attempts. "That's it," she said, quiet wonder in her voice. "That's exactly the memory."
