Mirror-lined walls reflected the studio's harsh fluorescent light as Anastasiya ran through the lift sequence one more time, counting the beats under her breath before the music even started. Opening night was three days away, and the transition still felt shaky.

Her dance partner, Roderick, adjusted his grip on her waist, testing the timing before nodding toward the sound booth for the music to start again. "Let's slow it down first," he said. "I want to feel exactly where I'm dropping the tempo before we go full speed."

Anastasiya nodded and reset to her starting position, watching Roderick's reflection in the mirror as they both found their marks. "On four this time," she said. "I kept rushing the approach in the last run, that's where we lost the timing."

The music started again, softer and slower than performance tempo, and this time Anastasiya's approach landed cleanly into Roderick's waiting arms, the lift rising smooth and controlled. She exhaled in relief as he lowered her back down with practiced control.

"That's it," Roderick said, grinning as he set her down fully. "Now let's do it three more times at that speed before we even think about pushing back to full tempo." Anastasiya laughed and wiped sweat from her forehead, already resetting to the starting mark.
