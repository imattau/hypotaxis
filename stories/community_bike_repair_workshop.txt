Chain grease coated Xiomara's hands as she demonstrated proper derailleur adjustment for the free community workshop, a small crowd of bike owners gathered around watching her explain each step. Saturday workshops always drew people needing basic repairs.

Her co-instructor, Torbjorn, worked with a workshop attendee at the neighboring stand, patiently walking the nervous first-timer through fixing his own flat tire under careful supervision. "You're doing great," he encouraged. "Just work the lever around slowly."

Xiomara finished her derailleur demonstration and moved to check on other attendees working through their own repairs, offering guidance wherever someone seemed stuck or confused. "Anyone need help with brake adjustment?" she called out.

Torbjorn's student successfully patched his tube and began reinflating the tire, clearly proud of the small accomplishment despite how basic the repair actually was for experienced cyclists. "First flat I've ever fixed myself," he said, grinning widely.

Xiomara smiled at the successful repair happening nearby and continued circulating through the workshop, satisfied with how many people were leaving today with new skills and newly functional bicycles. "This is exactly why we do these," she said to Torbjorn.
