Wood smoke curled from the brick oven's chimney as Guillermina fed another split log into the firebox, building the intense heat needed before tonight's bread could go in for baking. The wood-fired oven took nearly four hours to reach proper temperature.

Her baking apprentice, Osei, checked the oven floor's temperature with an infrared thermometer, calling out readings as the brick slowly absorbed the accumulating heat from the burning wood. "Floor's at four hundred and climbing," he said. "Getting close to ready."

Guillermina added another log and adjusted the fire's position slightly, working to ensure even heat distribution across the oven's entire floor before she began raking out the coals. "We want four fifty before I clear it," she said. "Even heat is everything with this style of baking."

Osei continued monitoring the temperature while Guillermina fed the fire steadily, both of them watching the readings climb toward the target temperature needed for the evening's bread. "Four thirty now," he reported. "Should hit target in another twenty minutes."

Guillermina nodded and began preparing her raking tools, anticipating the moment when she'd clear the coals and begin loading the first batch of carefully proofed loaves into the glowing oven. "Almost time," she said. "This is always my favorite part of the whole process."
