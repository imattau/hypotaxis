Dye-stained fingers worked the awl through thick leather as Cassius punched a steady row of stitch holes along the bag's edge, mallet tapping rhythmically against the tool's handle. The custom order had specific dimensions, and he'd already remade the front panel once to get it right.

His apprentice, Guinevere, waxed a length of thread nearby, pulling it through a block of beeswax repeatedly until it ran smooth between her fingers. "How many holes on this row?" she asked, counting the marked points along the leather's edge.

Cassius glanced over without pausing his work, the awl's rhythm barely breaking as he answered. "Forty-two on this panel," he said. "Count twice before you start stitching, a miscount means unpicking half an hour of work."

Guinevere counted the holes carefully a second time, confirming the number before threading her needle with the freshly waxed thread. "Forty-two confirmed," she said, positioning her needle at the first hole and beginning the saddle stitch pattern he'd taught her.

Cassius finished his own row of holes and set the awl aside, watching Guinevere's stitches with a critical but approving eye as she worked steadily down the panel. "Good tension," he said. "You're getting faster too. Should have this bag finished by tomorrow afternoon."
