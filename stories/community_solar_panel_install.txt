Her installation partner, Cornelius, connected the electrical wiring between panels, following the circuit diagram carefully to ensure the system would function safely once fully activated. "Wiring's looking clean," he said. "Should pass inspection without any issues."

Beatrix finished securing the last panel and moved to double-check all the mounting points, wanting to ensure the entire array could withstand the region's occasional severe weather. "Everything's secure," she said. "Let's get the inverter connected next."

Cornelius carried the inverter toward its designated mounting location, checking the specifications one final time against the installation manual's requirements before beginning the connection process. "This should be the last major component," he observed.

Beatrix helped him mount and connect the inverter, both of them working carefully through the final steps that would bring the community center's new solar array online. "Almost ready to flip the switch," she said. "This building's about to get a lot greener."
