Ice axes bit sharply into the frozen waterfall as Rosalind kicked her crampon points into the blue ice, ascending the vertical pitch with careful, methodical movements. The frozen cascade offered some of the region's best ice climbing once temperatures dropped low enough.

Her climbing partner, Fitzwilliam, belayed from below, feeding rope steadily while watching her progress up the ice for any signs of the placements looking questionable. "Looking solid," he called up. "That last screw placement sounded good."

Rosalind paused to place another ice screw, testing its hold with a firm tug before continuing her ascent through the increasingly steep section of frozen waterfall. "This section's more brittle than the last pitch," she called down. "Watch for ice chips."

Fitzwilliam adjusted his position slightly to avoid the falling ice chips from her tool placements, keeping his eyes on her progress while managing the rope's steady feed. "Copy that," he said. "You're maybe twenty feet from the anchor point."

Rosalind pushed through the final steep section and reached the anchor, clipping in securely before calling down for Fitzwilliam to begin his own ascent. "Belay's on," she called. "Come on up, the ice quality gets better after this next section."
