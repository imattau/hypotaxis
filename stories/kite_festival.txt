Wind gusted steady across the beach as Cordelia unraveled the last of her stunt kite's line, checking each handle for tangles before the festival's timed competition began. Colorful kites already dotted the sky above the crowded shoreline, dipping and weaving in practiced patterns.

Her flying partner, Ezra, held the kite steady above his head, waiting for her signal as the announcer's countdown crackled over the loudspeaker system nearby. "Thirty seconds," he said. "Remember the sequence, we open with the mirrored loops before the synchronized dive."

Cordelia nodded and took her position, feeling the wind's steady pull against the lines already taut in her gloved hands. "Ready when you are," she said. "Let's give them something worth watching this year."

The countdown reached zero, and Ezra released the kite into the waiting wind, both of them working the lines in careful coordination as the kite climbed and looped through its opening sequence. The crowd's murmur rose into scattered applause as the twin kites mirrored each other perfectly through the first maneuver.

Cordelia guided her kite into the synchronized dive, timing it precisely against Ezra's matching movement as both kites plunged toward the sand before pulling up at the last possible moment. The crowd erupted into applause, and Cordelia allowed herself a quick grin before refocusing on the routine's next sequence.
