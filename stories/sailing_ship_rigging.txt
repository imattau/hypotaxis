Salt-crusted rope burned against Ignatius's palms as he hauled the tall ship's mainsail line, adding his strength to the crew's coordinated pull while the ship's bosun called the rhythm from the deck below. The historic vessel's annual maintenance always required all hands.

His fellow crew member, Persimmon, climbed the ratlines above him, checking each connection point along the standing rigging for signs of wear that winter storage might have caused. "Found some chafing on the topgallant shroud," she called down. "Needs new service wrapping."

Ignatius finished securing his line and looked up toward where she'd indicated, squinting against the sun to assess the damage she'd spotted from her higher vantage point. "Add it to the list," he called back. "We'll need the bosun's approval before touching the standing rigging."

Persimmon continued her methodical inspection along the yard, testing each shroud's tension while documenting her findings for the ship's maintenance log that would guide the crew's coming weeks of work. "This old ship needs constant attention," she called down.

Ignatius nodded in agreement and moved to help with the next line, the historic vessel's demanding maintenance schedule a small price for keeping her sailing after over a century on the water. "Worth every hour," he said. "She's earned the care."
