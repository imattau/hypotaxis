Rain streaked the diner's front window as Jules slid into the corner booth across from her old college roommate, the vinyl seat creaking beneath her. A waitress dropped off two cups of coffee without being asked and vanished again toward the counter.

"You said it was important," Priya said, wrapping both hands around her mug for warmth. "You've been weird on the phone all week." Jules stirred her coffee slowly, staring at the small whirlpool she'd created rather than meeting her friend's eyes.

"I'm moving to Portland," she said finally. "The job offer came through yesterday." Priya set her mug down carefully, studying Jules's face for a long moment before responding.

"That's the job you didn't think you'd get," Priya said slowly. "The one you've wanted since senior year." Jules nodded, finally looking up, and Priya saw the mixture of excitement and guilt written plainly across her features.

"I start in three weeks," Jules said. "I know we just signed the apartment lease together. I know the timing is terrible." Priya reached across the table and squeezed her hand before she could keep apologizing.

"You're not allowed to feel guilty about this," Priya said firmly. "We'll figure out the lease. This is the job you've talked about for four years." Outside, a car splashed through a puddle at the curb, and Jules let out a breath she seemed to have been holding since she'd sat down, her shoulders finally loosening.
