Pipe after pipe stood ready on Cornelius's workbench as he adjusted each one's voicing, shaping the metal lips that determined the exact tonal character this particular rank would contribute to the cathedral organ's overall sound. Voicing demanded decades to master properly.

His apprentice, Xiomara, played reference notes on a portable keyboard, helping Cornelius compare each adjusted pipe's tone against the standard he was working to achieve across the entire rank. "This one still sounds a touch bright," she said.

Cornelius examined the pipe she'd flagged, making a minute adjustment to its lip before testing it again against the reference tone she continued providing. "Better," he said. "Voicing work is all about these tiny, tiny adjustments."

Xiomara played the reference note again, listening carefully alongside Cornelius as the adjusted pipe's tone now blended more seamlessly with its neighbors in the developing rank. "That matches beautifully now," she confirmed.

Cornelius set the finished pipe aside and reached for the next one waiting in the long row still requiring his careful attention, the tedious but essential work continuing toward the rank's eventual completion. "Two hundred more to go," he said. "Job security, at least."
