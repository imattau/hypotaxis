Goggles fogged slightly as Percival crouched at the starting line, thumbs hovering over the transmitter's sticks while he mentally rehearsed the track's tightest gate sequence one final time. The regional qualifier had drawn a bigger crowd than expected, spectators packed along the netting.

His race mechanic, Guinevere, made a final check of his drone's battery connection, tightening a prop nut she'd noticed was slightly loose during his last practice run. "Battery's fresh, props are tight," she said. "You're clear to fly whenever they call the start."

Percival nodded without opening his eyes, still visualizing the course's technical middle section where he'd crashed twice during yesterday's practice sessions. "This time I'm not rushing the double gate," he said. "Smooth beats fast through that section."

The starting signal sounded, and Percival's drone shot forward through the first gate in a blur, Guinevere watching the live feed on a nearby monitor alongside the anxious crowd of spectators. His flying looked clean through the early gates, controlled despite the course's increasing difficulty.

Percival threaded the double gate cleanly this time, and Guinevere pumped her fist as his drone crossed the finish line with the fastest lap time of the qualifying round. "That's how you do it," she said, already reaching to swap his battery for the next heat.
