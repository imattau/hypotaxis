Late afternoon sun slanted through the studio windows as Rosalind swept the last of the day's clay dust from the floor, the workshop finally quiet after a long, productive session. Her apprentice, Desmond, stacked the cleaned tools into their designated drawer.

"Good day's work," Rosalind said, glancing at the row of finished pieces drying on the shelf. Desmond nodded and closed the drawer, satisfied with how much they'd accomplished together before the light finally faded outside.
