String lights crisscrossed above the reception tent as Naomi smoothed the front of her bridesmaid dress, glancing nervously at the folded index card in her trembling hand. Laughter and clinking glasses filled the warm evening air around her.

Her best friend Iris, the bride, caught her eye from across the room and gave an encouraging thumbs up before turning back to accept another round of congratulations from an elderly relative. Naomi took a steadying breath and made her way toward the small stage where the band had just finished their set.

The best man, a broad-shouldered man named Curtis, handed her the microphone with a reassuring wink. "You've got this," he murmured, stepping back to let her take the spotlight. Naomi's hands shook slightly as she unfolded her index card, the words she had practiced a dozen times suddenly feeling foreign.

"Iris and I met in the worst dorm room on campus," she began, and a ripple of laughter spread through the crowd. Iris covered her face with both hands, shaking her head, but she was grinning behind her fingers. Naomi continued, voice growing steadier with each sentence, watching her friend's eyes grow glassy with tears across the room.

By the time she raised her glass for the final toast, the whole tent had gone quiet except for the soft rustle of napkins being dabbed against damp eyes. Iris crossed the room the moment Naomi stepped down, pulling her into a fierce hug as the band struck up a slow song behind them.
