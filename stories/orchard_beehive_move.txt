Moonlight lit the orchard rows as Balthasar sealed the last hive entrance with foam strips, working quickly before the colony fully woke to the disturbance. Moving bees had to happen at night, once every forager had returned home to sleep.

His hired hand, Rosamund, backed the truck carefully between the rows, tailgate lowered and ready to receive the sealed hives once Balthasar finished securing them. "Three more to go," he called quietly. "Keep the headlights off, it just agitates them."

Rosamund killed the lights and climbed down to help lift the first hive onto the truck bed, the low hum from inside the box audible even through the sealed entrance. "Heavier than I expected," she grunted, adjusting her grip before they set it down gently among the others.

Balthasar returned to the row for the next hive, moving with careful, unhurried steps despite the mosquitoes swarming around his headlamp's glow. "Almost done," he said. "Then we've got a two-hour drive before we can set them up at the new orchard."

Rosamund wiped sweat from her brow despite the cool night air and helped guide the final hive into place, securing the load with ratchet straps before climbing into the truck's cab. "Let's get moving," she said. "I want these girls settled before sunrise."
