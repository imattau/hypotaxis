Vegetable-tanned leather lay measured across Petronella's workbench as she cut another camera strap to length, checking the width consistency that professional photographers specifically valued in quality handmade equipment. Custom straps demanded exact tolerances.

Her apprentice, Anselm, prepared the hardware attachments, polishing the solid brass rings that would connect the strap to the camera's lug points once the leather work was fully completed. "Rings are polished and ready," he said.

Petronella examined the cut strap's edges, confirming they matched her exacting standards before beginning the edge-finishing process that would give this piece its professional appearance. "Nice clean cut," she said.

Anselm watched her burnish the edges, absorbing the technique that transformed rough-cut leather into something smooth and polished without any visible tool marks along the strap's length. "That edge looks incredible already," he observed.

Petronella finished the edge work and reached for the brass hardware, beginning the final assembly that would complete this particular custom strap commission. "Almost done," she said. "This one's going to serve someone well for years."
