Headlamp beams cut through the darkness as Osborne squeezed through the narrow passage, radio crackling with updates from the rescue team stationed at the cave's entrance far behind him. A group of stranded hikers had been trapped by a flash flood two chambers deeper, and time wasn't on anyone's side.

His rescue partner, Guadalupe, followed close behind, checking their rope line and calling out distances as they progressed deeper into the winding passage. "Two hundred meters to the junction," she said. "Water level readings from the last team should still be current."

Osborne paused at a tight squeeze and angled his gear pack sideways to clear it, breathing steadily to keep his composure in the claustrophobic space. "Almost through," he called back. "Chamber should open up right after this section, according to the survey map."

Guadalupe pushed through behind him a moment later, both of them emerging into a wider chamber where distant voices finally became audible over the sound of dripping water. "Over here!" a hiker's voice called out, relief evident even through the echo of the stone walls.

Osborne moved toward the voices quickly, Guadalupe right behind him with the medical kit, both scanning their lights across the chamber until the stranded group came into view, huddled together on a rocky ledge above the rising water.
