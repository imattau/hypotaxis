Colored glass tesserae covered Beauregard's workbench as he set another piece into the wet mortar bed, building the elaborate garden fountain's decorative mosaic pattern one careful placement at a time. The commission required thousands of individual pieces.

His apprentice, Marguerite, sorted through the sorted tesserae bins, selecting specific shades of blue that would create the water feature's flowing wave pattern according to Beauregard's detailed design sketch. "Found the exact blues you need," she said.

Beauregard took the offered pieces and began placing them along the wave's curve, checking the color gradient against his reference sketch to ensure the flowing effect he'd envisioned actually translated into the finished piece. "These work perfectly," he said.

Marguerite continued sorting while Beauregard worked, occasionally offering pieces before he even asked as she developed an intuitive sense for what the pattern would need next. "Getting good at anticipating you," she observed, handing over another handful.

Beauregard smiled and continued setting the wave pattern, grateful for an apprentice who'd developed genuine artistic instinct rather than just following mechanical instructions. "You're getting there," he said. "Soon you won't need me telling you what comes next."
