Lead came glinted under scaffolding lights as Theodosia guided the finished stained glass panel into the church's waiting window frame, checking alignment carefully before the final securing could begin. Months of careful work led to this installation day.

Her installation partner, Balthazar, mixed the glazing compound that would seal the panel permanently into its stone frame, working to the exact consistency that would provide both security and weatherproofing. "Compound's ready," he called down.

Theodosia confirmed the panel's alignment one final time, satisfied with how the afternoon light already caught the glass's rich colors even before the installation's completion. "Perfectly aligned," she said. "Let's get this secured before we lose the light."

Balthazar applied the glazing compound methodically around the panel's edges, working carefully to ensure complete coverage without any gaps that might compromise the installation's long-term weatherproofing. "Compound's going in clean," he reported.

Theodosia stepped back once Balthazar finished, watching afternoon sunlight stream through the newly installed panel and scatter colored light across the church's stone floor. "That's exactly the effect we wanted," she said. "Worth every hour of work."
