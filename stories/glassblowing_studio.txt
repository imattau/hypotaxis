Molten glass glowed orange at the end of the blowpipe as Cassiopeia rotated it steadily over the workbench, gravity pulling the gather into a slow, even bulb beneath her practiced hands. The furnace roared behind her, its heat pressing against her face even from several feet away.

Her studio partner, Thorsten, stood ready with the jacks, watching her rhythm closely so he'd know exactly when to step in and help shape the piece. "Looking good," he said. "Nice and even, no cold spots forming on that side yet."

Cassiopeia carried the glowing bulb to the marver and rolled it smooth against the steel table, cooling the surface just enough to control the shape without losing the workability underneath. "Get the blowpipe ready," she said. "I want to puff it before it stiffens up too much to move."

Thorsten passed her a fresh breath of air through the tube while she rotated the pipe, watching the bulb expand slowly into a rounder, more defined form under the combined heat and pressure. Sweat beaded on both their foreheads despite the studio's open bay doors letting in the cooler evening air.

By the time the piece was fully shaped and ready for the annealer, both of them stepped back to admire the swirling blue and amber pattern trapped within the glass. "That one's a keeper," Thorsten said, already reaching for the tools to start the next piece.
