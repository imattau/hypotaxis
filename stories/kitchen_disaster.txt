Priya stared in horror as the cake batter erupted out of the mixing bowl and across the kitchen counter, spattering the wall behind the stove. She had turned the mixer on high without checking the setting first, and now flour dust hung in the air like fog.

Her roommate Dev wandered in from the living room, took one look at the scene, and burst out laughing. "I thought you said you'd made this cake a hundred times," he said, leaning against the doorway with his arms crossed.

"I have!" Priya wiped a smear of batter off her cheek with the back of her hand, glaring at the mixer as if it had betrayed her personally. "The setting must have gotten bumped in the cabinet."

Dev grabbed a dish towel and started wiping down the wall, still grinning. "Your sister's birthday party starts in two hours. You sure you don't want to just buy a cake from the bakery down the street?"

"Absolutely not," Priya said, already scraping the surviving batter back into the bowl. "She specifically asked for my cake. I am not showing up with a store-bought one." She cracked two more eggs into a fresh bowl, this time keeping one hand hovering warily over the mixer's power switch.

Dev tossed the ruined towel into the sink and picked up a spare whisk. "Fine. But I'm helping, and if this batter ends up on the ceiling too, we're ordering pizza and telling her the oven broke." Priya swatted his arm with a wooden spoon, but she was smiling now, flour still dusted through her hair.
