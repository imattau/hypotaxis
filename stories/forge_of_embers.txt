Sora hammered the last curve into the blade, sparks scattering across the dark forge. Sweat dripped from her brow onto the anvil, hissing against the hot metal. She had promised the village elder this sword would be finished before the frost came, and the frost was already creeping down from the mountains.

An old man limped through the forge doorway, leaning heavily on a gnarled staff. His name was Torvald, and he had trained Sora since she was a child. He studied the half-finished blade without speaking for a long moment. "The edge is uneven," Torvald said, tapping it with one crooked finger. Sora set down her hammer, frustration plain on her face. "Then teach me to fix it," she said, "before it's too late." Torvald smiled faintly and reached for a smaller hammer of his own.
