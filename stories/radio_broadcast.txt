Red "ON AIR" light glowed steady above the studio door as Josephine adjusted her headphones, glancing at the clock counting down to the top of the hour segment. Her hands hovered over the soundboard, ready for the transition out of the current song.

Her producer, Ignatius, sat behind the glass in the control room, holding up three fingers through the window to mark the countdown before her live segment began. "Traffic report's cued after your intro," he said through her earpiece. "Keep it tight, we're running two minutes behind already."

Josephine nodded and watched the song's waveform on her screen taper toward silence, cueing her microphone the instant the last note faded. "Good afternoon, you're listening to ninety-four point three," she said smoothly, voice shifting into the warm, practiced cadence of a decade behind the mic.

Ignatius gave a thumbs up through the glass as her intro rolled smoothly into the scheduled traffic segment, exactly on cue despite the earlier delay. He scribbled a quick note on his clipboard and reached for the next segment's cart, already thinking two steps ahead in the broadcast.

Josephine glanced through the glass and caught his eye during a brief musical bed, mouthing a quick thank you before turning her attention back to the live board in front of her. The show rolled on, seamless to any listener unaware of how close they'd come to running behind.
