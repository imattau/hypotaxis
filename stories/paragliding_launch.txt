Wind streamed steadily up the ridge as Octavia laid out her paraglider's canopy across the grass, checking each line methodically before clipping into her harness. The launch site offered a clean view down the valley, thermals already visible rippling the distant treetops.

Her flying instructor, Barnaby, stood nearby watching the windsock's steady lean, gauging the conditions before giving his approval for the launch. "Wind's clean and steady," he said. "Good cycle coming through in about thirty seconds, get ready to run."

Octavia positioned herself at the canopy's center, feeling the fabric stir behind her as the wind cycle began building toward the moment Barnaby had described. "Ready," she called back, gripping the brake toggles with practiced familiarity.

Barnaby watched the wind carefully and gave the signal, and Octavia began her running launch, feeling the canopy inflate and lift overhead as her feet left the ground entirely. The valley opened up beneath her, ridge and trees shrinking as she caught the first thermal's gentle lift.

Barnaby watched from below, radio in hand, tracking her ascent as she circled higher into the afternoon sky. "Beautiful launch," he radioed up. "You're climbing nicely, ride that thermal as long as it holds."
