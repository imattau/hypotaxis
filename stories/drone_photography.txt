Rotors hummed steadily as Anselm guided the drone higher above the canyon rim, watching the live feed on his controller for the exact framing he'd been chasing all morning. Golden hour light was fading fast, and he had maybe ten more minutes before the shot lost its warmth.

His assistant, Philippa, checked the battery percentage on a second monitor, calling out updates as the drone continued its slow ascent toward the planned altitude. "Eighteen percent remaining," she said. "You've got about six more minutes of flight time before we need to bring it back."

Anselm nodded without looking away from the screen, fine-tuning the drone's position with small adjustments to the controller sticks. "Almost there," he said. "I want that ridge line right at the frame's lower third before the light drops completely."

Philippa kept her eyes on the battery indicator, ready to call an early landing if the numbers dropped faster than expected during the maneuver. "Fifteen percent," she said. "Whatever you're getting, get it now."

Anselm captured the final sequence of shots and immediately began the descent, guiding the drone back toward their position with practiced, efficient control inputs. "Got it," he said, exhaling in relief as the drone touched down safely on the canyon rim beside them.
