Molten glass gathered bright orange at Desmond's blowpipe as he shaped another holiday ornament, working the material through its spherical form before the seasonal craft fair's approaching deadline. Handblown ornaments demanded quick, confident technique.

His studio assistant, Anastasiya, prepared the next color frit, measuring the specific glass powder combination that would create this ornament's intended swirled color pattern once properly incorporated. "Frit's measured and ready," she said.

Desmond rolled the gathered glass through the prepared frit, watching the colors begin melting into the surface before returning to the furnace for another heat to smooth the incorporation. "These colors are blending beautifully," he said.

Anastasiya watched him continue shaping the ornament, absorbing the subtle breath control and rotation that transformed a simple glass gather into a perfectly spherical, evenly walled holiday decoration. "How do you keep it so round?" she asked.

Desmond continued his careful shaping, explaining the constant rotation and gravity awareness that prevented the molten glass from sagging unevenly during the demanding forming process. "Never stop turning," he said. "Gravity never takes a break either."
