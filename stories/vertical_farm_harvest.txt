Osei adjusted a few environmental settings based on his monitoring, fine-tuning the growing conditions to maintain the consistent quality that had built the farm's reputation with local restaurants. "Climate's stable across all racks," he reported.

Anastasiya finished her harvest round and began preparing the empty trays for their next planting cycle, satisfied with another productive day in the tightly controlled growing environment. "Let's get these replanted," she said. "No downtime in vertical farming."
