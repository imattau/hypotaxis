Flames roared from the small propane kiln as Anselm checked the glowing pots inside, timing the raku firing's rapid heat cycle that would produce the dramatic crackled surfaces this technique was known for. Raku demanded quick decisions under intense heat.

His firing partner, Cordelia, prepared the reduction chamber filled with combustible materials, ready to receive the red-hot pots the moment Anselm pulled them from the kiln for the crucial post-firing treatment. "Chamber's loaded and ready," she said.

Anselm opened the kiln and extracted the first glowing pot with long tongs, moving quickly to transfer it into Cordelia's waiting reduction chamber before the piece cooled too much for proper effect. "Here it comes," he called out urgently.

Cordelia sealed the chamber the instant Anselm placed the pot inside, trapping the smoke that would create the technique's signature crackled black and metallic surfaces through rapid reduction. "Sealed," she confirmed. "Now we wait and hope."

Anselm retrieved the next pot from the kiln while the first continued its dramatic transformation in the smoking chamber, the raku process's controlled chaos exactly as unpredictable as always. "Never gets less exciting," he said, grinning despite the heat.
