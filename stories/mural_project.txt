Spray cans rattled as Ximena shook another color before laying down a broad sweep of blue across the community center's brick wall, the outline of the mural already taking shape after weeks of planning. Traffic hummed past on the street behind her, occasional honks of encouragement from passing cars.

Her mural partner, Desmond, worked on the scaffolding above, filling in the upper section with careful strokes of a paint roller, pausing occasionally to check his section against the reference sketch taped nearby. "The color's reading a little too dark up here," he called down. "Might need to blend in more of that lighter blue."

Ximena stepped back to survey both sections together, comparing the transition zone where their two areas would eventually meet. "You're right, let's mix a batch to bridge it," she said, already reaching for her palette to test a new combination against the wall.

Desmond climbed partway down the scaffolding to compare colors directly, holding a test swatch up against both sections until they found a shade that blended seamlessly. "That's it," he said, climbing back up with the new mixture to continue his section.

By sunset, the mural's central figures had emerged fully from the brick, vibrant against the neighborhood's usual gray backdrop, drawing a small crowd of curious onlookers. Ximena capped her spray can and stepped back to take in the full piece, exhausted but proud of what they'd built together over the past month.
