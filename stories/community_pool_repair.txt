Chlorine fumes hung faintly in the air as Reginald knelt beside the pool's filtration room, tracing a stubborn leak back toward the main pump housing before the facility opened to the public in two hours. Summer's opening weekend couldn't afford a closed pool.

His maintenance partner, Filomena, held a flashlight steady over the pump, watching for the telltale drip Reginald was hunting through the tangle of pipes. "There," she said, pointing toward a fitting weeping steadily near the pump's base. "Right at that union joint."

Reginald reached in carefully and confirmed the leak's source, already mentally cataloging which wrench size he'd need to address the failing seal. "Cracked gasket," he said. "Good thing we keep spares, this would've been a disaster during opening weekend."

Filomena passed him the replacement gasket from the parts cabinet, watching as he began draining pressure from the line before attempting the repair. "Pool opens in ninety minutes," she said. "Think we'll make it?"

Reginald worked quickly but carefully, seating the new gasket and tightening the union joint with practiced efficiency despite the ticking clock. "We'll make it," he said, testing the repair before giving Filomena the signal to restore full pressure to the system.
