Crampons bit into blue ice as Sigrid led the rope team across the glacier's upper section, ice axe planted with each careful step around the crevasse field marked on yesterday's reconnaissance. Morning light caught the ice in brilliant flashes, beautiful but disorienting against the featureless white expanse.

Her climbing partner, Barnabus, followed a rope length behind, occasionally probing the snow ahead with his axe when the terrain looked suspicious for hidden crevasses. "Snow bridge up ahead looks solid," he called forward. "But let's test it before committing the whole team."

Sigrid approached the snow bridge cautiously and probed it methodically, finding solid resistance beneath the surface before signaling the rest of the team to proceed one at a time. "Solid," she confirmed. "Cross single file, keep the rope taut between us."

Barnabus crossed carefully behind her, testing his footing with each step despite Sigrid's confirmation, old habits from a near-miss two seasons ago still influencing his caution. "Clear," he called back to the rest of the team waiting on the other side.

Sigrid continued forward once everyone had crossed safely, checking her map against the visible landmarks ahead before adjusting their course slightly toward the day's planned camp. "Two more hours to camp," she said. "Let's keep moving before this wind picks up any further."
