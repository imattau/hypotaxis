Steam rose from the boiling water bath as Guillermina lowered another tray of shaped dough into the lye solution, the brief dip that would give the pretzels their distinctive dark, glossy crust. Bavarian tradition demanded this exact process, no shortcuts.

Her apprentice, Percival, shaped the next batch of dough into the traditional pretzel twist, working to match the precise proportions that gave properly made pretzels their characteristic appearance. "My twists are getting more consistent," he observed.

Guillermina lifted the boiled pretzels from the lye bath with a slotted spoon, arranging them carefully on the baking sheet before sprinkling coarse salt across their glossy surfaces. "Definitely improving," she said. "Your third batch was noticeably better than your first."

Percival finished shaping his current batch and brought them over for Guillermina's inspection, hoping to hear that his technique had continued improving with practice. "These look almost professional," she said, genuine approval in her voice.

Guillermina slid the salted pretzels into the hot oven, setting the timer for the precise baking time that would produce the perfect combination of chewy interior and crisp, dark crust. "Twelve minutes," she said. "Then we finally get to taste the results of your progress."
