Guitar case open and half-filled with coins, Amadeus finger-picked through his set list as commuters streamed past the subway platform, most barely glancing his way during the evening rush. The acoustics down here were better than most people realized, if you knew how to use them.

A stranger paused nearby, leaning against a support pillar to listen rather than rushing to catch the next train. "You've got a good voice for this space," she said once he finished the song. "I'm Odalys. Do you write your own stuff or mostly covers?"

Amadeus smiled and adjusted his guitar strap, grateful for the rare listener who actually stopped moving. "Bit of both," he said. "That last one was mine. Covers pay better down here though, people recognize the songs."

Odalys dropped a few bills into the open case and settled against the pillar, clearly in no hurry despite the crowd's steady flow around her. "Play another original," she said. "I want to hear more of what's actually yours."

Amadeus obliged, launching into a slower song he'd been refining for months, and for the first time that evening felt like he was playing for someone instead of just the passing crowd. Odalys closed her eyes and listened, the subway's rumble fading into background noise beneath the melody.
