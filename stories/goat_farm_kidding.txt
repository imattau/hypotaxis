Straw crunched under Petronilla's knees as she knelt beside the laboring doe, watching carefully for the first signs of the kid's hooves emerging. Middle-of-the-night kidding season meant broken sleep for weeks, but she wouldn't miss this part for anything.

Her hired hand, Barnaby, held a flashlight steady over the stall, ready to assist if the birth became difficult, though the doe seemed to be managing fine on her own so far. "Hooves are showing," he said quietly. "Looks like a normal presentation, nose right behind them."

Petronilla nodded and kept her hands ready but didn't intervene, trusting the doe's own rhythm as the contractions continued steadily. "Let her work," she said. "We only step in if something looks wrong, she's done this three times before."

The kid slipped free in one smooth motion, and Petronilla immediately cleared its nose and mouth while the doe twisted around to begin licking her newborn clean. Barnaby lowered the flashlight and let out a breath he hadn't realized he was holding.

"Healthy little doeling," Petronilla said, watching the kid attempt its first wobbly steps toward its mother. "Let's give them some time to bond, then we'll do the usual checkup once she's steady on her feet."
