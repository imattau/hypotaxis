Propane burners roared to life as Fenwick fired the envelope, the giant balloon slowly rising and filling with hot air against the pale predawn sky. Frost still clung to the grass in the launch field, though the morning promised to warm quickly once the sun cleared the tree line.

His ground crew chief, Odessa, held the crown line taut, watching the balloon's shape carefully for any sign of an uneven fill that could cause problems during inflation. "Looking good," she called out. "Give it one more good burn and we should be ready to stand her up."

Fenwick fired the burner again, the balloon's massive shape rising further off the ground as the basket began to tip upright under the shifting weight. "Standing up now," he said, adjusting his stance as the basket righted itself with a gentle thump against the grass.

Odessa released the crown line once the balloon stood fully upright, jogging over to help the passengers climb into the basket for the morning's flight. "Weather's perfect," she said. "Light winds, clear visibility, you couldn't ask for a better morning to fly."

Fenwick made his final checks and gave Odessa a thumbs up as she released the tether lines, the balloon lifting smoothly and silently into the brightening sky above the frosted field. Odessa watched it climb, already radioing ahead to the chase vehicle to begin tracking their flight path.
