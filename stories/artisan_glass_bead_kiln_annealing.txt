Freshly formed glass beads glowed orange as Seraphina carefully transferred them from her torch into the kiln's waiting mandrel rack, beginning the critical annealing process that would prevent the beads from cracking as they cooled. Proper annealing demanded patience.

Her studio assistant, Ottokar, programmed the kiln's cooling schedule, setting the precise temperature curve that would allow internal stresses within the glass to release gradually over many hours. "Schedule's programmed and ready," he said.

Seraphina finished loading the last beads from today's session, checking each one's placement to ensure proper heat circulation throughout the kiln's interior during the lengthy cooling process. "Everyone's loaded properly," she confirmed.

Ottokar closed the kiln door and started the programmed cycle, watching the display confirm the schedule had begun exactly as intended for this batch of delicate handmade beads. "Cycle's started," he reported. "Twelve hours until we can check them."

Seraphina nodded and began cleaning her workstation, satisfied with another productive session despite the long wait still ahead before these particular beads would be fully cooled and ready for stringing. "Worth the wait," she said. "Rushed annealing ruins everything."
