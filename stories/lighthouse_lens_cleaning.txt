Salt residue clouded the Fresnel lens's brass framework as Percival climbed the narrow ladder toward the lamp room, cleaning supplies balanced carefully as he ascended the lighthouse's spiral stairs. Monthly maintenance kept the historic light functioning for passing ships.

His assistant keeper, Rosalind, prepared the specialized cleaning solution below, measuring the exact ratio that wouldn't damage the lens's delicate glass prisms accumulated over the tower's century of service. "Solution's ready," she called up the stairwell.

Percival reached the lamp room and began the careful cleaning process, working section by section across the massive lens's curved surface to remove the salt spray's accumulated residue. "This section's particularly crusted," he called down. "Storm season really does a number on this glass."

Rosalind climbed up to help with the larger cleaning task, bringing fresh cloths and additional solution for the lens's remaining unclean sections that Percival hadn't yet addressed. "Halfway through," she observed, examining the lens's improving clarity.

Percival finished the final section and stepped back to admire the lens's restored brilliance, satisfied with how the light would now shine clearly for tonight's ships navigating the treacherous coastline. "Ready for tonight," he said. "Another month before we do this again."
