Glass vials clinked softly as Seraphine arranged her testing strips in a careful row, each one dipped in a different combination from the morning's blending session. The small perfumery smelled of a dozen competing florals, sharp and sweet all at once.

Her apprentice, Quillon, sniffed each strip in sequence, jotting brief notes on a pad before moving to the next. "Number four is close," he said. "But the bergamot's fading too fast, it's gone by the time you get to the base notes."

Seraphine took the strip from him and inhaled slowly, considering the fragrance's evolution over several long seconds before responding. "You're right," she said. "We need a fixative to slow that top note down. Try adding a touch more of the amber base."

Quillon returned to the blending bench and measured out a small amount of amber tincture, adding it carefully to a fresh testing vial before mixing it thoroughly. "This should hold the bergamot longer without overwhelming the florals underneath," he said.

Seraphine dipped a fresh strip into the new blend and waited for it to dry slightly before bringing it to her nose again, her expression shifting into something like satisfaction. "That's it," she said. "Now we let it rest for a day before deciding if this is the final formula."
