Loose dirt scattered as Bartholomew shaped another berm along the trail's descending switchback, packing the soil firm with his shovel before testing the angle with a quick practice roll of loose gravel. The new trail needed to be ready before the club's spring opening.

His trail-building partner, Rosalind, worked further up the slope, clearing roots and rocks from the line he'd need to complete the next section of flowing singletrack. "This root system's stubborn," she called down. "Might need the mattock to fully clear it."

Bartholomew finished packing his berm and climbed up to help her with the stubborn roots, examining the tangled system before deciding on the best approach for removal. "Cut it in sections," he said. "Trying to pull the whole thing at once will just tear up more trail than we want."

Rosalind worked the mattock through the roots methodically, following his suggestion while Bartholomew braced the surrounding soil to prevent unnecessary erosion during the extraction. "Almost through," she said, breathing hard from the physical effort.

Bartholomew helped clear the final loosened roots and inspected the newly cleared trail line, satisfied with how naturally it now flowed into the berm he'd shaped below. "Trail's really taking shape," he said. "Should be rideable within another two weekends."
