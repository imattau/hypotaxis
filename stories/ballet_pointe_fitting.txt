Satin ribbons draped over Isolde's shoulder as she laced the new pointe shoes, testing her weight carefully on the studio's sprung floor before committing to a full rise. Her old pair had finally worn through after months of daily rehearsal.

Her fitter, Barnaby, knelt nearby checking the shoe's fit around her arch, pressing gently to gauge whether the box provided enough support for her particular foot shape. "How does that feel through the demi-pointe?" he asked. "Any pinching at the sides?"

Isolde rose slowly onto pointe, testing her balance and the shoe's give beneath her weight before answering his question honestly. "A little snug at the toes," she said. "But the arch support feels much better than my old pair."

Barnaby made a small note about the fit and reached for a slightly wider size to compare, wanting to be certain before she committed to a full box of the current fitting. "Let's try this size too," he said. "Better to know now than after a full rehearsal."

Isolde changed into the wider pair and rose again, immediately noticing the improved comfort without any sacrifice to the support she needed for her more demanding choreography. "This one," she said. "Definitely this one."
