Whey drained steadily from the cheese press as Odalys checked the pressure gauge, adjusting the weight on today's batch of wheels according to the recipe's precise specifications for this particular aged variety. Cheesemaking rewarded patience above all else.

Her apprentice, Cassius, prepared the cheesecloth for the next wheel, folding it precisely to ensure even pressure distribution once this batch joined the others already pressing under the increasing weight. "This wheel's ready for the press," he said.

Odalys examined his cloth preparation, checking the fold pattern before approving the wheel for pressing alongside the others already under the gradually increasing weight schedule. "Perfect folding," she said. "Add it to station three."

Cassius positioned the new wheel in its designated press station, adjusting the initial light weight that would gradually increase over the coming hours according to the batch's specific pressing schedule. "Station three loaded," he confirmed.

Odalys checked all the active press stations one final time, satisfied with how this batch of wheels was progressing toward the density and moisture content this particular cheese style required. "Good pressing day," she said. "These will age beautifully."
