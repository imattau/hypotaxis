Fluorescent light flickered along the subway platform as Theo checked his watch for the third time, the screech of an arriving train echoing from the tunnel ahead. Rush hour crowds pressed close around him, phones out, headphones in.

A woman a few feet away dropped a stack of papers, and they scattered across the platform in the sudden gust from the incoming train. Theo crouched instinctively to help gather them, catching one page before it could blow onto the tracks.

"Thank you," the woman said, breathless, scooping up the rest with quick, practiced movements. "I'm already late enough without losing my whole presentation." Theo handed over the page he'd caught, glancing briefly at the header before she tucked it away.

"Good luck in there," he said, stepping back as the train doors hissed open in front of them. The crowd surged forward, and Theo found himself pressed shoulder to shoulder with strangers as he found a handhold near the door.

Across the car, the woman had found a seat and was reorganizing her papers with visible relief, smoothing out a creased corner. She caught Theo's eye once through the crowd and offered a small, grateful smile before returning her attention to the presentation notes spread across her lap, the train rattling onward into the tunnel's darkness.
