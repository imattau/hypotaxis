Ridge wind streamed steady across the launch platform as Maximilian ran his preflight checklist one final time, hands moving methodically across the glider's control bar and cables before committing to the jump. Ten years of flying hadn't dulled the pre-launch nerves, just made them familiar.

His launch assistant, Theodora, held the glider's nose steady against the gusting wind, watching his checklist progress while keeping her own eyes on the windsock's steady lean. "Wind's holding clean," she said. "You've got a good window whenever you're ready."

Maximilian nodded and gripped the control bar firmly, positioning himself for the running launch off the platform's edge toward the valley stretching out below. "Ready," he said. "Let go on my count. Three, two, one."

Theodora released her grip exactly on his count, and Maximilian sprinted forward off the platform, the glider catching air immediately as his feet left solid ground for open sky. The wing responded smoothly to his weight shift, banking gently as he found the ridge's steady lift.

Theodora watched him climb higher along the ridge line, already radioing his position to the ground crew tracking his flight path through the valley below. "Clean launch," she reported. "He's climbing nicely, looks like a good flying day out there."
