Rotor wash flattened the grass as Captain Wilhelmina hovered the helicopter steadily above the training field, maintaining precise position while her hoist operator prepared for today's rescue simulation exercise. Mountain rescue teams trained constantly for real emergencies.

Her hoist operator, Balthazar, checked the cable and harness one final time before signaling readiness to lower the rescue swimmer toward the simulated victim positioned below. "Cable's clear," he reported over the intercom. "Beginning lower."

Wilhelmina held the hover steady despite the challenging wind gusts, trusting her instruments and years of experience while Balthazar guided the rescue swimmer's descent toward the ground below. "Holding position," she confirmed. "Take your time."

Balthazar watched the swimmer reach the simulated victim and begin securing them for extraction, calling out the progress while monitoring the cable tension throughout the delicate procedure. "Victim secured," he reported. "Beginning extraction."

Wilhelmina maintained her precise hover as Balthazar hoisted both rescue swimmer and simulated victim back toward the helicopter, the training exercise proceeding exactly as their extensive practice had prepared them for. "Beautiful hoist," she said. "Textbook extraction."
