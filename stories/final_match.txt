Coach Adeyemi paced the sideline as the clock ticked under two minutes, her arms crossed tight against her chest. The score was tied, and her team had the ball deep in their own half after a scrappy turnover.

Number seven, a wiry midfielder named Priya Malhotra, called for the pass with her hand raised, sprinting into open space along the touchline. Her teammate Faro threaded the ball between two defenders, and Priya trapped it cleanly, cutting inside with a burst of speed that left one defender stumbling.

"Shoot from there and I will bench you myself!" Adeyemi shouted from the sideline, though there was no real heat behind it. Priya grinned despite herself and instead squared the ball across the box toward Faro, who had drifted unmarked to the far post.

The goalkeeper dove desperately as Faro's header sailed toward the top corner. For a heartbeat the entire stadium seemed to hold its breath, the ball spinning against the floodlights. It clipped the underside of the crossbar and dropped over the line before the keeper could scramble back.

The whistle blew for full time almost immediately after, and Priya was buried under a pile of teammates before she could even raise her arms in celebration. On the sideline, Adeyemi allowed herself a rare, wide smile, clapping slowly as the substitutes streamed past her onto the pitch.
