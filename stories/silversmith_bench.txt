Silver dust drifted from Cordwainer's file as he shaped the ring's band against his bench pin, checking the curve's symmetry against a steel mandrel every few strokes. The custom engagement ring commission needed to be flawless, and he'd already rejected two earlier attempts.

His apprentice, Theodosia, prepared the soldering station nearby, arranging her torch and flux with the careful precision he'd drilled into her over the past year of training. "Should I start heating the flux pot?" she asked. "Or are you still shaping the band?"

Cordwainer held the band up to check its roundness against the light before answering, satisfied with the shape after this latest round of filing. "Ready for soldering," he said. "Let's get this seam closed properly before we move to setting the stone."

Theodosia lit her torch and adjusted the flame to the precise size Cordwainer had taught her, applying flux to the seam before beginning the careful soldering process under his watchful supervision. The silver flowed cleanly into the joint, seam disappearing beneath the bright flash of heat.

Cordwainer inspected the finished seam under his loupe once it cooled, running a fingernail along the join to check for any remaining gap. "Perfect seam," he said. "Now for the part that actually terrifies me, setting a stone this valuable."
