Salt spray whipped across the deck as Genevieve trimmed the mainsail tighter, feeling the small sloop accelerate through the choppy harbor water toward the first marker buoy. The regatta's opening leg had them in third place, closer to the lead boats than she'd expected.

Her crew member, Thaddeus, hiked out hard over the rail to keep the boat flat, calling out wind shifts as he scanned the water ahead for the best angle to the mark. "Puff coming from the left," he shouted. "Ease the main slightly, we can point higher through this gust."

Genevieve eased the sheet exactly as he suggested, feeling the boat respond immediately as it accelerated cleanly through the wind shift without losing its heel angle. "Good call," she said. "We're gaining on the boat ahead, keep watching for more shifts."

Thaddeus kept his eyes forward, calling out small adjustments as they closed the gap toward the second-place boat rounding the marker just ahead of them. "Mark's coming up fast," he said. "Prepare to jibe, we'll need clean speed through the turn."

Genevieve called the jibe and worked the boat smoothly through the maneuver, the sail snapping across cleanly as they rounded the mark with barely a boat length between them and second place. "Now we chase," she said, trimming in hard for the next leg's upwind beat.
