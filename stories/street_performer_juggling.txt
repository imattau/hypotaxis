Sunlight glinted off spinning clubs as Alonzo worked through his juggling routine in the town square, a small but growing crowd gathering around the chalk circle marking his performance space. Saturday afternoons always drew the biggest audiences, tourists and locals mixing near the fountain.

His performing partner, Wilhelmina, called out the next trick's name theatrically, building anticipation before Alonzo attempted the five-club cascade he'd only recently mastered. "Watch closely," she announced to the crowd. "This one took him three years to learn."

Alonzo took a steadying breath and launched into the sequence, clubs spinning through the air in a blur while he maintained the rhythm despite the pressure of the watching crowd. Wilhelmina moved among the spectators, encouraging applause and keeping the energy building throughout the routine.

The fifth club joined the pattern cleanly, and gasps rippled through the crowd as Alonzo sustained the full cascade for nearly thirty seconds before bringing it to a controlled finish. Wilhelmina immediately gestured toward the upturned hat at the circle's edge, grinning at the enthusiastic applause.

"Give generously," she called out, moving through the crowd with the hat while Alonzo caught his breath and prepared for the next trick. Coins and bills dropped steadily into the hat as people lingered, clearly eager to see what he'd attempt next.
