Mortar tubes stood in precise rows across the barge as Cornelius double-checked each wiring connection against his choreographed sequence, the harbor's evening light fading fast toward tonight's celebration. Every connection had to be perfect before the crowds gathered along the shoreline.

His pyrotechnic partner, Guinevere, worked down the opposite row, cross-referencing each shell's placement against the master firing sequence taped to her clipboard. "Row seven's wired and tested," she called over. "Moving to row eight now."

Cornelius finished testing his own row and glanced at the darkening sky, calculating how much daylight remained before they needed to clear the barge for the safety inspection. "We're on schedule," he said. "Inspector's due in twenty minutes, let's make sure everything's labeled clearly."

Guinevere double-checked her wiring diagram against the physical setup, making a small correction to one connection that had been slightly mislabeled during the afternoon's rigging. "Good catch on my part," she said. "That would have thrown off the finale sequence."

Cornelius nodded and began the final walkthrough of the entire barge, checking each connection point methodically before the safety inspector's imminent arrival. "Everything checks out," he said. "This is going to be a good show tonight, I can feel it."
