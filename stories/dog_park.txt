Morning fog still clung low to the grass as Winnie unclipped her elderly retriever's leash near the dog park's entrance, watching him lumber off toward a cluster of familiar regulars. The air smelled of wet earth and the coffee she'd forgotten to finish before leaving the house.

A stranger nearby was struggling to untangle his own dog's leash from around a bench leg, muttering apologies to no one in particular. "He does this every single time," the man said, glancing up as Winnie approached to help. "I'm Callum, by the way. This disaster is Biscuit."

Winnie laughed and knelt to work the leash free from the other side, and within a few seconds the excitable little terrier bounded off to join the retriever in the open field. "Rookie mistake tying him that close to the bench," she said, straightening up. "Give it a few months, you'll learn all the tricks."

They ended up walking the park's loop together, dogs weaving ahead of them through the fog, occasionally circling back to check that their owners were still following. Callum asked question after question about training, clearly new to the whole routine, and Winnie found herself enjoying the excuse to talk about something she loved.

By the time the fog burned off and the sun broke fully over the tree line, both dogs had collapsed happily in a muddy puddle near the fence, thoroughly pleased with themselves. Winnie and Callum exchanged a resigned look before heading over to begin the inevitable cleanup together.
