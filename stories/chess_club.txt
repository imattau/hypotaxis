Fluorescent lights hummed over the community center's folding tables as Benji set up his pieces with the careful precision of someone who had done this a thousand times before. Across the board, his opponent, an elderly woman named Corinne, was already deep in thought before either of them had made a move.

"You're new to the club," Corinne said, not looking up from the board. "I've seen you watching the advanced matches from the corner for three weeks now." Benji flushed slightly and nodded, sliding his first pawn forward with a hand that trembled just a little.

"I didn't want to embarrass myself," he admitted. "Everyone here plays like they've been doing this since birth." Corinne allowed herself a small smile and countered his move without hesitation, her fingers moving with practiced economy across the worn wooden pieces.

The game stretched on for nearly an hour, other matches finishing around them as small crowds gathered to watch the unlikely pairing. Benji found himself forgetting to be nervous, absorbed instead in the shifting patterns of attack and defense unfolding between them.

When Corinne finally cornered his king with a quiet, almost gentle checkmate, she reached across the table to shake his hand. "Best game anyone's given me in months," she said. "Same time next week?" Benji grinned and began resetting the board before she'd even finished the sentence.
