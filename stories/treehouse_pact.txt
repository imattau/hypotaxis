Sunlight filtered through the leaves as eight-year-old Milo climbed the last wooden rung nailed into the oak tree, scraping his knee against the bark in his hurry. His best friend Ana was already inside the treehouse, arranging a row of mismatched action figures along the windowsill.

"You're late," Ana said without looking up, though she scooted over to make room for him on the plywood floor. "I already started the meeting without you." Milo rolled his eyes and dropped down beside her, pulling a crumpled paper bag of gummy candies from his pocket.

"I brought snacks," he said, holding the bag out like a peace offering. Ana took a handful immediately, her irritation forgotten as quickly as it had appeared. Outside, their neighbor's dog barked twice at something in the yard below, then fell silent again.

"We need a secret handshake," Ana announced suddenly, standing up so fast she nearly bumped her head on the low ceiling. "Real clubs have secret handshakes." Milo considered this with the utmost seriousness, chewing thoughtfully on a gummy worm.

"Okay, but it has to have a hand slap and a fist bump," he said. "And a spin at the end." Ana nodded solemnly, holding out her hand to begin practicing right away. They spent the next twenty minutes perfecting the sequence, dissolving into giggles every time one of them fumbled the spin and nearly toppled off the treehouse's narrow platform.
