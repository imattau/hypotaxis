Polished chrome gleamed under the auction hall's bright lights as Bartholomew walked the restored roadster one final time, checking for any last-minute detail that might affect its value before bidding began. Three years of restoration work led to this exact moment.

His restoration partner, Guinevere, stood near the registration desk, watching the growing crowd of serious collectors filtering into the auction hall for today's featured lots. "Good turnout," she said. "I recognize at least three collectors who specifically came for this model."

Bartholomew ran a cloth over an already spotless section of chrome, nervous energy needing somewhere to go before the auctioneer called their lot number. "Hope the reserve isn't set too high," he said. "We put everything into this restoration."

Guinevere checked the auction program again, confirming their lot's position in the day's schedule while trying to project more confidence than she actually felt about the outcome. "Lot's coming up next," she said. "Let's go find seats where we can actually see the bidding."

Bartholomew took one last look at the roadster before following her toward the seating area, the culmination of three years of weekend work now entirely out of his hands. "Whatever happens," he said, "we built something beautiful either way."
