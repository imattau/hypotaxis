Crystal prisms caught workshop light as Beauregard wired another cascade section into the elaborate chandelier's growing framework, working toward the specific arrangement the historic hotel's restoration had specifically requested. Custom chandeliers demanded structural precision.

His apprentice, Marisol, polished individual crystal pieces before handing them over, removing any fingerprints or dust that might diminish their sparkle once the chandelier was finally illuminated. "These prisms are polished and ready," she said.

Beauregard took the polished crystals and continued wiring them into position, checking the cascade's visual balance against his original design sketch throughout the demanding assembly process. "This section's looking beautiful," he said.

Marisol watched him continue the intricate wiring work, absorbing the subtle balance considerations that prevented the heavy crystal arrangement from appearing lopsided or structurally unstable once fully assembled. "How many crystals does this piece need total?" she asked.

Beauregard calculated based on the remaining unwired sections, arriving at a number that represented many more hours of careful, detail-intensive work before this ambitious restoration project would be complete. "About three hundred more," he said.
