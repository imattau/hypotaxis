Strings of paper lanterns crisscrossed the town square, swaying gently above the crowd as drums echoed from the stage at the far end. Yui wove between food stalls, balancing two skewers of grilled dumplings while searching the sea of faces for her friends.

She spotted Emi first, waving both arms from beside a fountain lit up in shifting colors. "Over here!" Emi called, laughing as a group of children ran past chasing a paper dragon puppet held aloft on poles.

"I got you one," Yui said, holding out a skewer as she reached her. Emi took it gratefully, still watching the dragon puppet weave through the crowd toward the stage.

A tall boy in a dark jacket approached from the opposite direction, hands in his pockets. "You made it," he said to Yui, a faint smile tugging at his mouth. "I thought you were working the late shift tonight."

"Kenji switched with me," Yui said. "Wouldn't miss this for anything." Behind them, the drumming built toward a crescendo, and the crowd began to press closer to the stage as the first fireworks cracked open in the night sky, painting the lanterns below in brief flashes of gold and red.

Emi grabbed Yui's sleeve, pulling her toward a clearer view. "Come on, we're missing it!" The three of them pushed through the crowd together, faces tilted up toward the bursts of color unfolding overhead.
