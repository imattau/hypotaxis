Wood chips flew steadily as Barnabus worked the roughing gouge against the spinning bowl blank, shaping the walnut into a rough form before the more delicate finishing cuts could begin. Lathe work demanded both strength and surprising delicacy.

His apprentice, Guinevere, watched the tool angle closely, absorbing the subtle technique that seemed to separate Barnabus's clean cuts from her own rougher attempts on the practice lathe nearby. "Your gouge angle looks different than mine," she observed.

Barnabus paused the lathe and demonstrated the angle more slowly, showing how the tool's bevel needed to rub against the wood before the cutting edge could engage properly. "Ride the bevel," he said. "That's the whole secret to clean cuts."

Guinevere returned to her own practice piece, attempting the technique he'd demonstrated, and felt the distinct difference as the tool engaged more smoothly than her previous attempts. "That's completely different," she said, genuinely surprised.

Barnabus smiled and returned to his own bowl, the roughing nearly complete before he could move toward the hollowing process that would create the bowl's final interior shape. "Now you're actually turning wood," he said. "Not just fighting it."
