Tiny locomotive wheels clicked softly against the track as Alphonse tested the newly wired signal block, watching the miniature train slow appropriately as it approached the crossing he'd spent all week rewiring. The club's basement layout had taken three years to build to its current scale.

His fellow club member, Persimmon, crouched beside the layout's mountain section, carefully painting individual leaves onto a scratch-built tree with a fine-tipped brush. "Signal's working perfectly now," she said, glancing up from her painting. "That crossing gave you trouble for weeks."

Alphonse nodded, satisfied, and reached for the controller to send another test train through the newly functional block. "Turned out to be a loose connection under the roadbed," he said. "Should have checked there first instead of rewiring the whole relay."

Persimmon returned to her tree, adding careful highlights of lighter green to suggest sunlight filtering through the canopy. "Looks good either way," she said. "The whole layout's coming together nicely for the open house next month."

Alphonse sent one more test train through the crossing, watching it glide smoothly past the now-functional signal without the stuttering that had plagued it for weeks. "Just the river section left to finish," he said. "Then we're actually ready for visitors."
