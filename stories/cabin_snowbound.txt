Snow piled thick against the cabin's shuttered windows as Astrid fed another log into the wood stove, sparks crackling up into the dark chimney. The storm outside had knocked out the power hours ago, leaving only the stove's orange glow and a single battery lantern.

Her brother Joel sat cross-legged on the floor nearby, methodically checking the contents of their emergency pack by lantern light. "We've got maybe two days of food if we're careful," he said, setting aside a stack of energy bars. "Water's not a problem, at least. We can melt snow."

Astrid closed the stove door and rubbed her hands together over its warmth. "The ranger station said the plows wouldn't reach this road until the storm breaks," she said. "Could be tomorrow, could be three days." Outside, wind howled against the cabin walls, rattling the shutters in their frames.

Joel zipped the pack shut and leaned back against the couch, exhaling slowly. "At least we're not stuck out there," he said, nodding toward the window. "Remember the Petersons' cabin? No wood stove at all." Astrid managed a tired laugh, pulling a wool blanket tighter around her shoulders.

A sudden gust rattled the chimney pipe, and both of them glanced up instinctively before the sound settled back into the storm's steady roar. "I'll take first watch on the stove," Astrid said, already reaching for another log. "You should try to sleep while it's still warm in here."
