Steam hissed from beneath the hood of the old station wagon as Beatriz coasted it onto the gravel shoulder, cutting the engine before it could sputter out entirely. Heat shimmered off the empty desert highway stretching in both directions.

Her cousin Mateo climbed out of the passenger seat, shading his eyes as he squinted down the road they'd already traveled. "I told you we should have gotten the radiator checked before we left," he said, though there was no real bite in it.

"You told me a lot of things," Beatriz said, popping the hood and jumping back as a cloud of steam billowed out. She waved it away with one hand, coughing, and peered doubtfully at the tangle of hoses and belts beneath. "None of which included how to fix an overheating engine in the middle of nowhere."

Mateo pulled his phone from his pocket and held it up, turning in a slow circle. "No signal," he announced. "Of course." He walked to the back of the wagon and dug through their bags until he produced a half-empty water bottle, holding it up triumphantly. "For the radiator, not for us. Probably."

Beatriz took the bottle from him, unscrewing the radiator cap carefully with a rag wrapped around her hand. Steam hissed out again, softer this time. In the distance, a battered pickup truck appeared as a shimmering speck on the horizon, and Mateo immediately began waving both arms over his head.
