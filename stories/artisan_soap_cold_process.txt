Lye solution steamed faintly as Philomena poured it slowly into the melted oils, stirring carefully behind her safety goggles while the mixture began thickening toward trace. The workshop smelled of eucalyptus and shea butter, warm despite the open window.

Her business partner, Torvald, measured the essential oil blend precisely, checking the recipe card taped to the counter before adding the fragrance to their prepared batch. "Fragrance is measured and ready," he said. "I used the exact ratio from last month's successful batch."

Philomena continued stirring with the immersion blender, watching the mixture thicken visibly with each pulse toward the consistency that indicated proper trace had been achieved. "Perfect timing," she said. "Pour it in now, before this sets up too thick to blend evenly."

Torvald poured the fragrance oil in a slow, steady stream while Philomena worked the blender through it, the mixture turning glossy and uniform as eucalyptus scent bloomed through the workshop. She poured the finished soap into the waiting mold.

"Four weeks to cure," Philomena said, tapping the mold against the counter to release trapped air bubbles before setting it on the curing rack. Torvald began cleaning their equipment, already discussing what scent combination they should test for next month's batch.
