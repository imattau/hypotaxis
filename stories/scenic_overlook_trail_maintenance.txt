Loose gravel crunched under Rosalind's boots as she cleared fallen branches from the scenic overlook trail, working to restore the popular hiking route after last week's severe windstorm damage. Weekend hikers would arrive expecting a clear path.

Her trail crew partner, Balthazar, sawed through a larger fallen log blocking the narrowest section of trail, working methodically to clear the obstruction without damaging the surrounding vegetation. "This one's bigger than it looked from a distance," he said.

Rosalind finished clearing her section of smaller debris and moved to help Balthazar with the log, dragging cleared sections off the trail into the surrounding forest. "Almost through," she said, checking his progress on the stubborn cut.

Balthazar finished the final cut and helped Rosalind clear the remaining sections, working together to restore the trail's original width for the weekend's expected foot traffic. "That should do it for this section," he said, wiping sweat from his brow.

Rosalind surveyed their completed work and glanced at her trail map, calculating how much further they needed to hike before reaching the next reported damage section. "Two more spots to check," she said. "Should have this whole trail cleared by afternoon."
