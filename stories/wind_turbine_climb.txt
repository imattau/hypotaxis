Wind roared against the tower as Theodora clipped her harness to the next rung, climbing steadily up the turbine's interior ladder toward the nacelle nearly three hundred feet above the ground. The gearbox inspection couldn't wait for calmer weather, not with the turbine already flagged for unusual vibration.

Her climbing partner, Ferdinand, followed a few rungs below, radio clipped to his shoulder relaying updates to the ground crew monitoring their ascent. "Halfway up," he called down. "Wind's picking up a bit, but still within our climbing limits."

Theodora paused at a rest platform to catch her breath, glancing out through a small access port at the wind farm stretching across the ridge below. "Almost to the nacelle," she said. "Let's keep moving, I want to get the inspection done before this wind gets any worse."

Ferdinand caught up to her at the platform, and together they continued the final stretch of ladder toward the access hatch at the top of the tower. "Gearbox should be right past this hatch," he said, already reaching for the tools clipped to his harness.

Theodora pulled herself through the hatch into the nacelle's cramped mechanical space, immediately spotting the source of the vibration in a loosened mounting bolt. "Found it," she said, relief clear in her voice. "Hand me the torque wrench, this should be a quick fix."
