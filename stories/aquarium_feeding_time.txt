Bubbles streamed upward as Ottoline descended into the massive tank wearing full scuba gear, mesh bag of fish secured to her weight belt for the daily feeding demonstration. Visitors pressed against the glass outside, cameras ready for the show.

Her dive partner, Bartholomew, remained topside on the platform, monitoring her air supply gauge readout and relaying updates to the aquarium staff coordinating the presentation below. "She's descending to feeding depth," he announced to the gathered crowd. "Watch for the sharks approaching from the left."

Ottoline reached the designated feeding zone and began distributing fish methodically, maintaining calm, deliberate movements despite the large reef sharks circling closer with each pass. The crowd's murmur rose as one shark glided within inches of her outstretched hand.

Bartholomew watched the feed from above, ready to signal an early ascent if anything looked concerning, though Ottoline's steady rhythm suggested everything was proceeding exactly as planned. "She's got about five more minutes down there," he told the crowd. "This is the best part of the show."

Ottoline finished distributing the last of the fish and gave a thumbs up toward the surface, beginning her controlled ascent as the crowd broke into enthusiastic applause. Bartholomew helped her out of the tank once she surfaced, both of them grinning at another successful feeding demonstration.
