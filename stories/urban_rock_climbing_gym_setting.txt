Colorful holds scattered across the gym floor as Anouk consulted her route-setting diagram, selecting the next piece for the overhanging wall's newest problem before climbing the ladder to bolt it into position. Monday reset days always meant a completely fresh set of routes.

Her fellow route-setter, Bartholomew, worked the adjacent wall, testing his own newly set problem by climbing through the sequence himself to verify the intended movement actually worked as designed. "This heel hook is brutal," he called down, hanging from an awkward position.

Anouk laughed and continued bolting her own hold into place, checking the angle carefully to ensure the movement she'd envisioned would actually be achievable for climbers attempting the route. "That's the point," she said. "Make them work for it."

Bartholomew dropped down from his test climb and made a small adjustment to one hold's rotation, wanting to fine-tune the sequence's difficulty before officially opening the route to gym members. "Better," he said, testing the adjusted movement again.

Anouk finished her own route and stepped back to evaluate the overall wall composition, satisfied with how the day's new problems created an interesting variety of movement styles. "Good reset," she said. "Members are going to have fun with these tomorrow."
