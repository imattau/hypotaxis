Kessa walked slowly toward the Mill, her boots sinking into the wet grass along the riverbank. She had not been back since the flood. The old waterwheel still turned, groaning with every rotation, moss creeping up its wooden spokes. She pushed open the door and stepped inside, dust catching the light from the windows.

Later that evening, Kessa returned to the Mill one last time before leaving the village. She stood at the water's edge, watching the wheel turn in the fading light, remembering everything that had happened there.
