Beam of light pierced the crystalline water as Bartholomew finned slowly along the guideline, reel paying out steadily behind him as he mapped the cave system's newest discovered passage. Cave diving demanded a discipline different from open water, every movement deliberate and controlled.

His dive partner, Wilhelmina, followed a body length behind, sketching the passage's dimensions onto a waterproof slate as they progressed deeper into the unmapped section. She signaled toward a side passage branching off unexpectedly from the main tunnel.

Bartholomew paused at the junction and checked his gas gauge, calculating whether they had enough remaining to explore the new branch before needing to turn back. "We've got maybe fifteen minutes before turn-pressure," he signaled. "Quick look, then we head back."

Wilhelmina nodded and followed him into the side passage, flashlight beam revealing striking formations that suggested this section had never been surveyed before. She sketched quickly, capturing the passage's unusual dimensions before their limited time ran out.

Bartholomew checked his gauge again and signaled the turn, both of them reversing course carefully along the guideline toward the cave's entrance and the surface waiting far above. "New passage logged," he thought, already planning next week's return trip to map it fully.
