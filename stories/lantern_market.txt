Kaito wound through the crowded night market, lanterns swaying overhead in the warm breeze. He clutched a small wooden box against his chest, glancing nervously over his shoulder. A woman selling roasted chestnuts called out to him, but he did not stop.

At the far edge of the market, Mei waited beneath a paper lantern shaped like a carp. She had been standing there for an hour, watching every face in the crowd. When she finally saw Kaito approaching, she stepped out from the shadow of a stall. "You're late," she said, crossing her arms. Kaito held out the wooden box without a word, his hands trembling slightly. Mei opened it slowly and gasped at what was inside.
