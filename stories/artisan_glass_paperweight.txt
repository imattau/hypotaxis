Molten glass gathered orange at the punty rod's end as Torvald layered colored canes into the developing paperweight, building the intricate floral pattern that would eventually be encased in clear crystal. Millefiori technique demanded precise timing.

His studio assistant, Persimmon, prepared the next batch of colored canes, arranging them in the specific pattern order Torvald's design called for this particular commission's complex floral arrangement. "Canes are sorted and ready," she said.

Torvald took the prepared canes and began arranging them around the developing weight's core, working quickly before the glass cooled too much to accept the delicate pattern properly. "These colors are working beautifully together," he said.

Persimmon watched his technique closely, absorbing the subtle timing and heat management that allowed such intricate patterns to form without the canes shifting or blurring together. "How do you keep everything so precisely placed?" she asked.

Torvald continued gathering clear glass over the pattern, encasing the floral design permanently within the paperweight's crystal dome before beginning the shaping process that would give it its final form. "Practice," he said. "And probably a thousand ruined attempts."
