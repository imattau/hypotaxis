Steel cables hummed faintly overhead as Valentina checked each carabiner on the trapeze rig one final time before the evening's performance, headset crackling with updates from the crew below. The big top's canvas ceiling stretched high above, ropes and rigging crisscrossing toward the peak.

Her rigging partner, Boaz, tugged hard on the safety net's edge, testing its tension methodically before giving Valentina a thumbs up from the ground below. "Net's solid," he called up. "Rig's looking good too, but double-check that center clamp before the flyers go up."

Valentina climbed higher on the rig and inspected the clamp closely, running her fingers along the metal to feel for any give or looseness in the connection. "Clamp's tight," she confirmed. "We're clear for the full routine tonight, no restrictions on the triple."

Boaz coiled the extra safety line and stowed it neatly out of the performers' path, glancing at the clock mounted near the entrance as the pre-show announcements began echoing through the tent. "Fifteen minutes to doors," he said. "I'll get the flyers briefed on the net check."

Valentina descended the rig quickly and met Boaz at the base, both of them running through the final safety checklist together before signaling the stage manager that everything was ready. "Let's put on a show," she said, already reaching for her own harness to begin warming up.
