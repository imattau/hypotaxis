Wet clay spun steadily beneath Zofia's palms as she centered another lump on the wheel, beginning the careful process of throwing a lidded jar's matching body and lid components. Fitted lids demanded exceptional precision throughout the throwing process.

Her apprentice, Radomir, measured the jar's rim diameter with calipers, recording the exact dimensions that would guide the lid's throwing to ensure a proper, satisfying fit once both pieces reached completion. "Rim's measured and recorded," he said.

Zofia continued throwing the jar's body, checking the wall thickness with practiced fingers before beginning to shape the shoulder that would eventually support the fitted lid. "This shoulder's looking nice and even," she said.

Radomir prepared to throw the lid separately, using the calipers' recorded measurements to ensure the flange would match the jar's rim precisely once both pieces reached the leather-hard stage for trimming and fitting.

Zofia finished the jar's body and set it aside to firm up, satisfied with how this particular piece was developing toward its eventual lid-fitting and glazing process. "Now we throw the lid," she said. "The trickiest part is still ahead."
