Dr. Alvarez knelt on the exam room floor, murmuring softly to calm the trembling terrier mix pressed against the corner. A fresh bandage was already wrapped around the dog's front paw, dark spots of blood seeping faintly through the gauze.

Her vet tech, Sanjay, held a clipboard steady while keeping one hand ready to assist if the dog bolted. "Owner said he got into some broken glass in the backyard," Sanjay said quietly. "No other injuries that she could see."

"Good girl," Dr. Alvarez murmured, running a gentle hand along the dog's trembling back before checking the bandage's edges. "The cut wasn't too deep, but I want to keep an eye on it for infection." She reached for a small flashlight and carefully checked the dog's eyes and gums next.

The dog let out a low whine and pressed closer against the wall, ears flattened. Sanjay crouched down slowly, offering the back of his hand for the dog to sniff before attempting to stroke behind its ears. "You're doing great, buddy," he said softly, and the dog's tail gave one hesitant wag.

Dr. Alvarez stood and made a few notes on the chart Sanjay held out for her. "Let's get him a cone and send him home with antibiotics," she said. "Owner should watch for swelling or discharge over the next few days." Sanjay nodded, already moving toward the supply cabinet while the terrier watched him go, tail thumping a little harder now against the tile floor.
