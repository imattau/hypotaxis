Sawdust billowed from the mill's main blade as Gunnar guided another log through the carriage, watching the fresh-cut lumber emerge in clean, even boards stacked automatically toward the sorting area. Monday mornings always started with the week's biggest log deliveries.

His mill partner, Ottilie, monitored the automated stacking system from the control booth, calling out adjustments whenever the lumber grade needed different sorting than the default settings allowed. "This batch is running high grade," she said. "Switching to the premium sort bin."

Gunnar nodded and adjusted the carriage speed slightly to match the higher-grade lumber's more careful handling requirements, wanting to avoid any unnecessary waste from this particularly nice batch of logs. "Good wood today," he said. "Whoever's supplying us found a solid stand."

Ottilie continued monitoring the sort as boards continued emerging steadily from the saw, tracking the day's total yield against their weekly production targets. "We're ahead of schedule," she said. "Should hit the week's quota by Thursday at this rate."

Gunnar guided the next log through and glanced at the growing stack of quality lumber accumulating in the premium bin, satisfied with the morning's productive start. "Let's keep this pace," he said. "Good batches like this don't come around every week."
