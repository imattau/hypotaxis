Yuna climbed the last few rungs of the fire escape and pulled herself onto the rooftop, breathing hard. The city stretched out below her in a haze of orange streetlights. She had not been up here since the summer before last.

Daichi was already waiting near the water tower, sitting with his knees pulled up to his chest. He did not turn around when she approached. "I didn't think you'd actually come," he said quietly. Yuna sat down beside him without answering, and for a long moment neither of them spoke. Then she pulled a folded piece of paper from her jacket pocket and held it out to him. "I made a promise," she said. "I keep my promises."
