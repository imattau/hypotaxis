Sunlight dappled through clear water as Beatrix swam along the transect line, clipboard slate in hand recording coral health at each marked interval on this section of the reef. Bleaching events over the past decade had made this kind of monitoring increasingly urgent for the research team.

Her dive partner, Alaric, photographed each coral colony methodically, documenting the survey site for comparison against next year's data collection. He signaled toward a particularly vibrant section of staghorn coral, clearly pleased at the sign of healthy growth.

Beatrix swam closer and recorded the colony's measurements, comparing its current size against the baseline data from three years prior with visible satisfaction at the growth rate. She gestured for Alaric to get a close-up shot before moving to the next marked point along the transect.

Alaric captured several angles of the healthy coral before following Beatrix toward the next survey point, checking his air gauge along the way to track their remaining bottom time. He tapped her shoulder and held up his gauge, signaling they had roughly fifteen minutes left.

Beatrix nodded and picked up her pace slightly, wanting to complete at least two more survey points before they needed to begin their ascent back toward the boat waiting above. She recorded one final measurement and gave Alaric a thumbs up, signaling it was time to head up.
