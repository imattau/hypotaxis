Cream swirled thick against the churn's paddle as Guinevere monitored the batch's consistency, checking the custard base's temperature before adding this week's featured flavor infusion. Small-batch ice cream demanded patience most commercial producers skipped entirely.

Her shop assistant, Torvald, prepared the toasted hazelnuts that would fold into the finished batch, chopping them to the precise size that would distribute evenly without overwhelming the delicate cream base. "These smell incredible toasted," he said.

Guinevere checked the churn's progress and nodded, satisfied with how the base had thickened toward the proper soft-serve consistency that indicated readiness for mix-ins. "Perfect timing," she said. "Let's fold in those hazelnuts now before it sets up too firm."

Torvald added the toasted nuts gradually as Guinevere folded them through the churning base, distributing them evenly without breaking down their pleasant crunch. "Looking good," he observed, watching the nuts disappear into the pale cream.

Guinevere finished the fold and began transferring the finished batch into storage containers, satisfied with another successful small-batch creation ready for tomorrow's shop opening. "This batch might be our best yet," she said, sealing the final container.
