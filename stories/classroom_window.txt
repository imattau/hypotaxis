Haru stared out the classroom window, barely listening to the teacher's voice drone on about the exam schedule. Rain streaked down the glass, blurring the baseball field where he used to practice every afternoon before his injury. His notebook lay open and untouched on the desk.

Behind him, Nozomi leaned forward and tapped his shoulder with the end of her pencil. She had noticed him staring outside for the third class in a row. "You're going to fail the exam if you keep doing that," she whispered, glancing at the teacher to make sure she hadn't been seen. Haru turned around slowly, surprised anyone had spoken to him at all this week. "I don't really care about the exam," he admitted quietly. Nozomi frowned and slid her own notebook across the aisle toward him. "Well, I do," she said. "So pay attention."
