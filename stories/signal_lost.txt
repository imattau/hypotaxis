Captain Reyes floated weightless in the observation deck, watching static crawl across the comms display. The station had gone silent six hours ago, and the silence was starting to feel like a countdown. Warning lights blinked slowly along the far wall, casting red shadows across her face.

An engineer named Petra pulled herself through the hatch, magnetic boots clanking against the deck plating. Grease streaked one side of her jaw where she had been working the reactor coupling. "The relay's fried," Petra said, breathless. "We're not getting a signal out through the main array." Reyes closed her eyes for a moment, steadying herself against the console. "Then we use the backup transmitter," she said. "Get it online. Now." Petra nodded and pushed off toward the corridor without another word.
