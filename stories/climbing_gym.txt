Chalk dust hung in the air as Zephyrine studied the overhanging route, tracing the sequence of holds with her eyes before chalking up for another attempt. The gym buzzed with the usual evening crowd, ropes swinging and climbers calling encouragement across the padded floor.

Her climbing partner, Osei, stood belay-ready at the base of the wall, rope looped through his device as he watched her chalk her hands one final time. "You had it last try," he called up. "Just commit to the deadpoint instead of hesitating at the crux."

Zephyrine nodded and stepped onto the wall, moving smoothly through the opening sequence before pausing at the same crux move that had shut her down twice already. She took a breath, then lunged for the sloping hold above, fingers barely catching the edge before her feet cut loose from the wall.

Osei held the rope steady, watching her feet scramble to find purchase again as she hung by fingertips from the small hold. "You've got it, reset your feet," he called, keeping his voice calm despite the tension in his grip on the belay device.

Zephyrine found a foothold and pulled through the final moves to the anchor, clipping in with a triumphant shout that drew a few claps from nearby climbers. She lowered slowly to the ground, grinning and shaking out her forearms while Osei coiled the rope and grinned back at her.
