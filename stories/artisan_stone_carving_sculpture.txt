Marble dust coated Anselm's protective goggles as he worked his chisel against the block's surface, slowly revealing the human form he'd envisioned trapped within the raw stone before beginning this commission months ago. Sculpture demanded patience above all else.

His apprentice, Wilhelmina, cleared accumulated dust from the working surface, keeping Anselm's view of the emerging form clear as he continued the painstaking process of removing unwanted stone. "The shoulders are really taking shape now," she said.

Anselm paused his chiseling to step back and examine the sculpture from a distance, checking the proportions against his original clay maquette before continuing the detailed carving work. "Good eye," he said. "Proportions are staying true to the model."

Wilhelmina returned to clearing dust as Anselm resumed carving, absorbing the subtle differences in chisel angle and hammer force that produced such controlled, deliberate stone removal. "How do you know exactly where to cut next?" she asked.

Anselm continued working, considering the familiar question that every apprentice eventually asked during their sculptural training under his guidance. "The form's already in there," he said. "I'm just removing what doesn't belong."
